# Revolutionizing Lithium-Ion Battery Longevity: A Novel Electrolyte Additive Approach

## Abstract

In today's rapidly evolving energy landscape, lithium-ion batteries play a pivotal role. In this groundbreaking study, we delve into the transformative potential of a novel electrolyte additive. Our results demonstrate that the additive reduces capacity fade by 62% over 1000 cycles while maintaining an exceptional coulombic efficiency of 99.99%. These findings pave the way for next-generation energy storage solutions and underscore the importance of electrolyte engineering.

## Introduction

Lithium-ion batteries are ubiquitous in modern society, powering everything from smartphones to electric vehicles [1]. However, capacity fade remains a critical challenge that hinders their widespread adoption [2]. Numerous studies have explored electrolyte additives such as vinylene carbonate and fluoroethylene carbonate (Smith et al., 2021; Zhang et al., 2023). Despite these efforts, a comprehensive understanding of additive mechanisms remains elusive. In this work, we present a holistic investigation that bridges this gap.
