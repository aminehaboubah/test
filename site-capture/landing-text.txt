FEATURES
Everything a paper is made of, kept

Not a text dump. Paper Parse understands the structure of a scientific document and carries it across formats.

Real equations

Display and inline formulas stay LaTeX — rendered with KaTeX in the reader, native equations in Word, compilable in TeX.

Faithful structure

Headings and numbering, theorems and proofs, lists with their own labels, footnotes, references and citations — as the source prints them.

Tables & figures

Spanning cells, captions and figure crops survive the trip, and diagrams LaTeX draws become images where nothing else can show them.

Any format to any format

PDF, Word and LaTeX in; PDF, LaTeX and Word out — in one or two columns and five journal templates.

Read, correct, write

A calm reader with outline and search, the original page beside the text — and a LaTeX editor with a live PDF, SyncTeX and an AI assistant.

Optional AI review

Turn it on for difficult PDFs: models check headings, clear figure debris and compare pages with the result. Off by default.

HOW IT WORKS
From file to finished paper in three steps
Drop your files

PDF, Word or LaTeX — several at once. Word and LaTeX are ready in about a second, a PDF in under a minute.

Read and fix

Open the paper in the reader, compare it with the original page and correct anything in place. Every change is kept as a version.

Export anywhere

Download it in the format you need, in the column layout and journal template you choose.
