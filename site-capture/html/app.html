How it works

Three steps, the same for every format.

1. Convert

Drop files above. Each one is parsed into headings, paragraphs, lists, equations, figures and tables. A PDF takes 20–60 s (more with AI review); Word and LaTeX take about a second.

To convert again — with AI review, or after an update — use Re-process in a document's ⋯ menu.

2. Read and correct

  • Open shows it in the reader: outline, search, themes, figure zoom.
  • For PDFs, click a page number to see the original page beside the text.
  • Edit fixes text and block types; the original conversion is kept.

3. Export

Choose Export, a format, columns and a journal template (article, IEEE, ACM, LNCS, Elsevier).
